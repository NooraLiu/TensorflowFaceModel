Photo Replication Experiment — 3D Camera Control Study

Purpose

This study investigates how people use different camera control methods (mouse-only vs. head+mouse) to navigate a 3D scene during a block-repositioning task. Results will be used for academic research.

What You Will Do

  • Complete two blocks of 3D camera control tasks (~12 trials each)
  • Use your webcam for head-pose detection in one condition
  • Fill out brief questionnaires after each block and at the end
  • Total duration: approximately 45–50 minutes

Data Collection

  • Task performance data (completion time, placement accuracy)
  • Webcam-derived head pose estimates — no video is recorded or transmitted
  • Survey responses (workload, usability, preference)
  • All data is stored locally and identified only by your participant ID

Your Rights

  • Participation is entirely voluntary
  • You may withdraw at any time without penalty by closing the browser
  • Data collected up to that point may be retained in anonymised form
